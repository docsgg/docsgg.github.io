Domains

How to use country-code top-level domains (ccTLDs) to confirm your location. Domains are one of the most reliable clues in GeoGuessr.

Europe

European Domains

Most European countries use their two-letter ISO code as their ccTLD. Some have unique domains or use second-level domains.

  • Albania: .al
  • Andorra: .ad
  • Austria: .at
  • Belarus: .by (Minsk only)
  • Belgium: .be
  • Bosnia and Herzegovina: .ba
  • Bulgaria: .bg
  • Croatia: .hr
  • Czechia: .cz
  • Denmark: .dk
  • Estonia: .ee
  • Finland: .fi
  • France: .fr
  • Germany: .de
  • Gibraltar: .gi
  • Greece: .gr
  • Hungary: .hu
  • Iceland: .is
  • Ireland: .ie
  • Isle of Man: .im
  • Italy: .it
  • Jersey: .je
  • Latvia: .lv
  • Liechtenstein: .li
  • Lithuania: .lt
  • Luxembourg: .lu
  • Malta: .mt
  • Monaco: .mc
  • Montenegro: .me
  • Netherlands: .nl
  • North Macedonia: .mk
  • Norway: .no
  • Poland: .pl
  • Portugal: .pt
  • Romania: .ro
  • Russia: .ru
  • San Marino: .sm
  • Serbia: .rs
  • Slovakia: .sk
  • Slovenia: .si
  • Spain: .es
  • Sweden: .se
  • Switzerland: .ch (or .swiss)
  • Turkey: .tr
  • Ukraine: .ua
  • United Kingdom: .uk (or .co.uk)
  • Åland Islands: .ax
Important: Switzerland uses .ch (Confoederatio Helvetica), not .swiss (though .swiss exists, .ch is far more common). Spain is .es, not .sp. United Kingdom uses .uk or .co.uk, not .gb.

Asia

Asian Domains

Asian ccTLDs are usually straightforward, but some have unique second-level domains or use different codes.

  • Bangladesh: .bd
  • Bhutan: .bt
  • Cambodia: .kh
  • Hong Kong: .hk
  • India: .in
  • Indonesia: .id
  • Iraq: .iq (limited coverage)
  • Israel: .il
  • Japan: .jp
  • Jordan: .jo
  • Kazakhstan: .kz
  • Kyrgyzstan: .kg
  • Laos: .la
  • Lebanon: .lb
  • Macau: .mo
  • Malaysia: .my
  • Mongolia: .mn
  • Nepal: .np
  • Oman: .om
  • Pakistan: .pk
  • Palestine: .ps
  • Philippines: .ph
  • Qatar: .qa
  • Singapore: .sg
  • South Korea: .kr
  • Sri Lanka: .lk
  • Taiwan: .tw
  • Thailand: .th
  • United Arab Emirates: .ae
Important: Japan uses .jp (and .co.jp). South Korea uses .kr (and .co.kr). Taiwan uses .tw, not .cn. Hong Kong uses .hk, not .cn.

Africa

African Domains

African ccTLDs are often the two-letter ISO code. Some countries have limited coverage.

  • Botswana: .bw
  • Eswatini: .sz
  • Ghana: .gh
  • Kenya: .ke
  • Lesotho: .ls
  • Madagascar: .mg
  • Malawi: .mw
  • Mali: .ml
  • Namibia: .na
  • Nigeria: .ng
  • Rwanda: .rw
  • Senegal: .sn
  • South Africa: .za
  • Tanzania: .tz
  • Tunisia: .tn
  • Uganda: .ug
Important: South Africa uses .za (Zuid-Afrika), not .sa (Saudi Arabia). Eswatini (formerly Swaziland) uses .sz.

North America & Caribbean

North American & Caribbean Domains

  • Bermuda: .bm
  • Canada: .ca
  • Costa Rica: .cr
  • Cuba: .cu
  • Curaçao: .cw
  • Dominican Republic: .do
  • Guatemala: .gt
  • Martinique: .mq
  • Mexico: .mx
  • Panama: .pa
  • Puerto Rico: .pr
  • United States: .us (or .com, .gov, .edu)
Important: The United States often uses .com or .gov, not just .us. Canada uses .ca, Mexico uses .mx.

South America

South American Domains

  • Argentina: .ar
  • Bolivia: .bo
  • Brazil: .br
  • Chile: .cl
  • Colombia: .co
  • Ecuador: .ec
  • Peru: .pe
  • Uruguay: .uy
Important: Brazil uses .br (and .com.br). Colombia uses .co (which can be confused with .com). Argentina uses .ar.

Oceania

Oceanian Domains

  • Australia: .au (and .com.au)
  • New Zealand: .nz (and .co.nz)
  • Papua New Guinea: .pg
  • Pitcairn Islands: .pn
  • Vanuatu: .vu
Important: Australia uses .au, New Zealand uses .nz. Don't confuse .au with .at (Austria).
