About GeoDocs

A community-driven, structured guide to help you master GeoGuessr — from basic meta to advanced regionguessing.

📖 What is GeoDocs?

GeoDocs is an open, collaborative project created by players for players. We collect, organize, and explain the most useful clues, metas, and strategies used in GeoGuessr — covering everything from country identification by road markings and architecture to climate zones and domain names.

Our goal is to make learning GeoGuessr easier, more structured, and more fun. Whether you're a beginner trying to tell countries apart or a seasoned dueler looking for advanced regionguessing tips, GeoDocs has something for you.

  • Structured: Content is organized by regions and categories.
  • Community-driven: Written and maintained by experienced players.
  • Practical: Focused on real, usable clues you can apply in-game.
  • Free: Accessible to everyone, no paywalls.
