Hemisphere

The equator splits the Earth into a northern and a southern half. Knowing which half you are in cuts your search in two.

Northern Hemisphere

Everything north of the equator.

Equator

The line at 0° latitude that splits the two halves.

Southern Hemisphere

Everything south of the equator.

Recognize the hemisphere in GeoGuessr

You don't need a map. The position of the sun tells you which half of the world you are in.

The rule

If the sun is in the south
You are in the northern hemisphere
If the sun is in the north
You are in the southern hemisphere

How to check

  1. Find the sunLook for the sun or the brightest patch of sky, even on a hazy day.
  2. Read the compassFace the sun and check the direction shown at the top of the screen.
  3. Apply the ruleSun in the south means northern hemisphere. Sun in the north means southern.

See it in real rounds

Sometimes this rule does not work

It is a strong clue, not a guarantee. Be careful in these cases:

  • You are in the tropicsBetween about 23° north and 23° south the sun can be almost overhead, or on either side depending on the season.
  • It is early or late in the dayNear sunrise and sunset the sun is in the east or west. The rule works best around midday.
  • The sun is not visibleOn cloudy days there is no clear sun to read. Use other clues instead of guessing.
  • You do not know the timeStreet View shows no clock, so the sun's height alone can fool you. Combine it with other clues.
