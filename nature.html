Nature & Climate

Identify climate zones and biomes to narrow down your location. Includes only countries with official Google Street View coverage.

🌴 1. Equatorial & Tropical

Vegetation: Dense multi-layered tropical rainforests. Huge variety of trees, lianas, epiphytes (orchids, ferns), palms. In seasonally wet areas — savannas (tall grasses and sparse trees like acacia or baobab).

Street View Countries:

Brazil Colombia Ecuador Peru Bolivia Indonesia Malaysia Philippines Cambodia Thailand Bangladesh Sri Lanka Kenya Tanzania Uganda Nigeria Ghana Senegal Madagascar

🌵 2. Arid / Desert

Vegetation: Sparse, xerophytic. Cacti, succulents, thorny shrubs, rare acacias. In semi-deserts (steppes) — low grasses and shrubs (scrub).

Street View Countries:

Egypt United Arab Emirates Qatar Israel Jordan Oman Australia (Outback) Namibia Botswana (Kalahari) USA (Arizona, Nevada) Mexico (North) Chile (Atacama)

🌿 3. Mediterranean

Vegetation: Hard-leaved evergreen forests and shrubs. The famous "maquis" (dense, low, thorny thickets). Trees with small, hard leaves (olives, cork oak, pines).

Street View Countries:

Spain Italy Greece Turkey Croatia France (South) Portugal USA (California) Chile (Central) South Africa (Cape Town) Australia (Perth, Adelaide)

🌳 4. Temperate / Deciduous

Vegetation: Broad-leaved forests that shed their leaves in autumn (oak, beech, maple, linden). Lots of herbaceous plants. Also includes temperate steppes/prairies (grasses, feather grass).

Street View Countries:

France Germany Poland Ukraine Russia (European) USA (East, Midwest) Japan South Korea China (East) Argentina (Pampas)

🌲 5. Boreal / Taiga

Vegetation: Coniferous forests (taiga). Spruce, pine, fir, larch. Huge, dense, dark forests. Bogs and swamps are common.

Street View Countries:

Canada USA (Alaska) Sweden Finland Norway Russia (Siberia)

❄️ 6. Tundra

Vegetation: No trees. Only mosses, lichens, low grasses, and dwarf shrubs (willows, birches). Permafrost is everywhere.

Street View Countries:

Canada (North) Norway (North) Iceland Russia (North) Greenland (Denmark)

⛰️ 7. Alpine / Mountain

Vegetation: Altitudinal zonation. Forests at the base, then shrubs, then alpine meadows (grasses and wildflowers), then bare rocks and snow.

Street View Countries:

Switzerland Austria Chile Peru Nepal Bhutan USA (Rockies) Canada (Rockies)
